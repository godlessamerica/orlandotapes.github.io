# Required Social Media Images for Orlando Tapes

To complete the SEO optimization, you'll need to create the following images and add them to the `/images/` folder:

## 1. orlando-tapes-cover.jpg
- **Purpose:** Main podcast artwork
- **Size:** 1400x1400 pixels (minimum for iTunes/Apple Podcasts)
- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Description:** Square podcast cover art featuring Orlando Tapes branding

## 2. orlando-tapes-og-image.jpg  
- **Purpose:** Open Graph image for Facebook, LinkedIn, and other social media
- **Size:** 1200x630 pixels
- **Format:** JPG
- **Description:** Should include Orlando Tapes logo, tagline, and imagery representing Orlando's music scene

## 3. orlando-tapes-twitter-card.jpg
- **Purpose:** Twitter Card image
- **Size:** 1200x675 pixels  
- **Format:** JPG
- **Description:** Similar to OG image but optimized for Twitter's aspect ratio

## 4. favicon.ico
- **Purpose:** Website favicon
- **Size:** 32x32 pixels (multi-size ICO file preferred)
- **Format:** ICO file
- **Description:** Small Orlando Tapes logo or "OT" initials

## Design Suggestions

### Visual Elements to Include:
- Orlando Tapes logo/text
- Microphone or audio waveform imagery
- Orlando skyline silhouette
- Music venue imagery (stage lights, crowd, etc.)
- Color scheme that reflects Orlando's music scene

### Taglines to Consider:
- "Orlando's Underground Music Scene Documented"
- "Live Music from Orlando, FL"
- "Discover Orlando's Music Scene"
- "Will's Pub • Stardust Coffee • Live Recordings"

### Color Palette Suggestions:
- Dark backgrounds with bright accent colors (common in music branding)
- Orlando-inspired colors (sunset oranges, blue skies)
- High contrast for readability across platforms
