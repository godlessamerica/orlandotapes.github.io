Featured Orlando Music Venues

Will's Pub

A cornerstone of Orlando's music scene, Will's Pub has been hosting live music for decades. Located in the Mills 50 district, this intimate venue is known for its diverse lineup of local and touring acts. From punk to indie rock, Will's Pub provides the perfect setting for discovering Orlando's underground music talent. The venue's intimate atmosphere creates an unmatched connection between artists and audience.

Orlando, FL - Mills 50 District

Featured Episodes: Youth To The Front series, featuring Kitty Kitty Meow Meow, Misspell, Catnap, Soft, and Dogsmiles

Stardust Coffee & Video

A unique combination of coffee shop and music venue, Stardust Coffee & Video provides an intimate setting for acoustic performances and experimental music in Orlando. Located near Winter Park, this venue champions local artists and creates a community space where music and coffee culture intersect. The venue's laid-back atmosphere makes it perfect for both established and emerging Orlando musicians.

Orlando, FL - Near Winter Park

Featured Episodes: S.M.O.P. and M.A.C.E. live performances

Uncle Lou's

The world famous Uncle Lou's on Mills Ave is the most important incubator of underground music in Orlando. This legendary venue has been a vital part of the city's DIY music scene, providing a space where experimental, punk, and indie artists can push boundaries and connect with passionate audiences. Uncle Lou's raw, intimate atmosphere captures the true spirit of Orlando's underground music community.

Orlando, FL - Mills Ave

Featured Episodes: Tiger Beat live performance

About Orlando Tapes

Orlando Tapes documents the vibrant underground music scene of Orlando, Florida. We capture live performances from iconic venues across the city, preserving the authentic sound and energy of local musicians. From the intimate stages of Will's Pub in the Mills 50 district to the unique atmosphere of Stardust Coffee & Video, our podcast showcases the diverse talent that makes Orlando's music scene truly special.

Each episode is carefully recorded and mixed to provide listeners with an immersive experience of Orlando's live music culture. Our focus on authenticity means you'll hear the real energy of live performances - the crowd, the venue acoustics, and the raw talent of Orlando's underground musicians. Whether you're a longtime Orlando music fan or new to the scene, Orlando Tapes is your gateway to discovering the city's most exciting musical talent.

Series: Youth To The Front

Our flagship series "Youth To The Front" highlights emerging bands and established acts from Orlando's music community. Recorded primarily at Will's Pub, this series captures the essence of Orlando's underground music scene, featuring bands across genres from punk and indie rock to experimental and electronic music.
